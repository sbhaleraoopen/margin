# Privacy Policy

**Effective from Margin's first public release.** Last updated October 3, 2026.

Margin is a calendar helper for iPhone and Mac, made by Sachin Bhalerao. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **everything stays on your device**. Margin has no account, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your calendar, your messages or anything else you put into the app.

## What Margin uses, and why

Margin asks for each permission only when a feature needs it. You can turn any of them off in Settings at any time.

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Calendars** | To show your events, find free time, warn about clashes, and add the events you confirm. | Read and written on your device through Apple's calendar system. Your calendars sync with whichever accounts you've set up (iCloud, Google and so on), as they already do. |
| **Reminders** | To show reminders on your days, count them as busy if you ask, and add a reminder when you choose a time. | Read and written on your device through Apple's Reminders system. |
| **Location** (approximate, only while using the app) | Only when you tap *Find on map*, so nearby places come first. | Used on your device to shape the Apple Maps search (see below). Never stored. |
| **Text you paste or share, and screenshots you choose** | To read the event out of them. | Read on your device. A screenshot's text is read by Apple's on-device text recognition; the image isn't kept. An unsaved draft is kept on your device until you save or clear it. |
| **Mail** (Mac app only, if you turn on the Mail scan) | To find events in your inbox and suggest them. | Read on your Mac, without changing anything. Found events wait in a calendar named "Suggested" in your own iCloud account until you add or dismiss them. Your "I'm going" answers to them, and senders you choose to mute, are saved as small records in that same calendar, so your Mac and iPhone agree. |

Events are read by Apple Intelligence's **on-device** model when your device has it, or by fixed rules built into the app when it doesn't. Either way, your text is not sent anywhere.

## The one thing that leaves your device

When you use **Find on map**, your search words, and your approximate area if you've allowed location, go to **Apple Maps** to find places. That search is handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Margin makes no other network requests.

## What's stored, and where

- **In your calendars and reminders:** only the events and reminders you choose to add. Each event Margin adds carries a short random code in its URL field, used to make sure a retried save never adds the same event twice. It contains no personal information. Anyone you share that calendar with can see it, as with any event field.
- **On your device only:** your settings (default calendar, calendar colors, which calendars are "only when I'm going," your answers to those events, except Suggested ones as described above), recent places you picked on the map, and an unsaved draft. Deleting the app deletes them.

Margin keeps no copy of your data anywhere else, and there is nothing for the developer to sell, share or hand over.

## Diagnostics (off unless you turn it on)

Margin has a hidden diagnostics mode for troubleshooting, turned on by tapping the version number in About seven times. While it's on, Margin keeps a short log on your device of the steps it took (for example "read the text, asked for a time, saved"), how long reading took, and any error codes. It never records the text of your events. The log leaves your device only if you tap **Share report** and send it yourself, for example by Mail or Messages. Turning diagnostics off deletes the log.

## Crash reports and beta feedback

Margin doesn't include any crash-reporting or analytics tools. If you've chosen to share data with app developers in your device's settings, or you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may pass crash reports, and any feedback you write, to the developer. These are controlled by Apple's settings and Apple's privacy policy, and are used only to fix problems.

## Children

Margin isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because Margin collects nothing, a change would only describe a new on-device feature, or a new network request, which would be named here before it ships.
