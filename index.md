# Margin

<p class="lede">Turn a message into a calendar event, and find time for the things you mean to do. Everything stays on your device.</p>

- **Paste, share or snap it.** Text, a screenshot, or Share from Mail becomes an event. You check it before it's saved.
- **Find a time.** Openings in your week, from the calendars you already use.
- **Private by design.** No account, no ads, no tracking. Events are read on your device.
